Sheaf records switches as fronts: you start a front for whoever is at the
front, and end it when they step back.

## See who's fronting

The **"Dashboard"** shows the current state at a glance. The
**"Currently fronting"** card lists each active front with the fronting
members and how long each has been at the front, plus the front's custom
status if one was set. The **"System"** card next to it counts your members
and **"Active fronts"**. When no front is active, the card reads
**"Nobody is fronting right now."**

## Start a front

1. Select **"Start front"** at the top of the dashboard. (The same button
   appears on the **"Fronts"** page.)
2. Pick who is fronting. The chips at the top of the dialog are quick picks:
   your pinned members and recent fronters. Below them is the full member
   list; archived members are not shown.
3. To record co-fronting, select more than one member. Every selected member
   goes on the same front entry.
4. Optionally add a note under **"Custom status (optional)"**, for example
   "at work". It is shown with the front wherever the front appears.
5. Leave **"End all current fronts"** checked to make this a switch: any
   active fronts end and the new one replaces them. Uncheck it to start this
   front alongside the ones already running.
6. Select **"Start"**.

<ThemeImage
	light={startFrontLight}
	dark={startFrontDark}
	alt="Start front dialog with quick-pick chips, group and tag filters, member search, the member list, custom status field, and the End all current fronts checkbox"
/>

If your system has groups or tags, filter rows appear above the member list.
Pick a group chip to narrow the list to that group and its subgroups, or
**"All groups"** to clear the filter; tag chips work the same way, and a
group filter and a tag filter combine. With more than eight members a
**"Search members…"** box also appears, matching against member names and
display names.

The default for **"End all current fronts"** comes from
**"End current fronts when starting a new one"** in Settings, under System.

If the exact same set of members already has an active front, Sheaf rejects
the duplicate and explains why in the dialog.

## End a front

In the **"Currently fronting"** card, select **"End"** next to the front.
Ending a front does not delete it; it closes the entry with an end time and
it moves into your history.

## Front history

Past fronts live on the **"Fronts"** page, which lists every entry under
**"History"** with start and end times, and supports editing and deleting
entries. See [Front history](/guides/fronting/history/) for details.

## See who's fronting

The Home tab greets you with your system name and shows a card for each
member currently fronting, with their avatar, name, pronouns, how long they
have been at the front, and the front's custom status if one was set. When
no front is active, the screen shows **"No one is fronting"** instead.

## Record a switch

1. Tap **"Switch"** in the bottom corner of the Home tab.
2. In the **"Select who's fronting"** sheet, tick everyone who is now at
   the front. The current fronters start out ticked, so recording a switch
   means unticking whoever is stepping back and ticking whoever is arriving.
   Ticking more than one member records co-fronting.
3. If your system has groups, the chips above the list narrow it to one
   group at a time; **"All"** clears the filter. (Android filters by group
   only, not by tag.) The **"Search members"** box matches against member
   names.
4. Leave **"End current fronts"** checked to make this a switch: the new
   front replaces the ones already active. Uncheck it to start the new
   front alongside them. The checkbox starts from your system's
   end-current-fronts default, the same setting the web app reads.
5. Optionally add a note under **"Custom status (optional)"**. It is shown
   with the front wherever the front appears.
6. Tap the confirm button at the bottom of the sheet; it shows how many
   members are selected.

## Quick switch

The **"Quick switch"** row above the bottom navigation lists your pinned
members first, then your most frequent recent fronters. Tapping a chip
switches to that member immediately, using your system's default for
ending current fronts. To choose explicitly, long-press the chip:
**"Switch (end current)"** ends the active fronts, and **"Add to front"**
starts the front alongside them.

## End a front

Long-press a member's card on the Home tab and confirm with **"Remove"**
in the **"Remove from front?"** dialog. This takes that member off every
front they are part of; a front with nobody left on it ends and moves into
your history.

Switches and front removals made while offline are queued and sync
automatically once you are back online.
