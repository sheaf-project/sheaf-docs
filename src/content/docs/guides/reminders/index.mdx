Reminders deliver through your notification channels: each reminder rides
one channel you have already set up, whether that is your own ntfy topic
or a channel someone else receives.

## Your reminders

The **"Reminders"** page lists every reminder with a summary of when it
fires, the channel it delivers through, and, for scheduled reminders, the
next fire time. Each row has **"Pause"** and **"Resume"** to stop and
restart a reminder without losing its setup (a paused reminder shows a
**"Disabled"** badge), plus edit and delete buttons.

## Create a reminder

You need at least one notification channel first; if you have none, the
page points you to [Front notifications](/guides/notifications/) to
create one.

1. Select **"New reminder"**.
2. Give it a **"Name"** for your list, plus the notification content:
   a **"Title"** and an optional **"Body (optional)"**.
3. Under **"Send via"**, pick the notification channel that delivers it.
4. Pick the **"Trigger type"**: **"Schedule"** for a recurring reminder,
   or **"After a switch"** for an automated one tied to front changes.
5. Confirm with Create.

## Scheduled reminders

A **"Schedule"** reminder repeats. Pick a **"Frequency"** of
**"Daily"**, **"Weekly"** (choose which **"Days"**), or **"Monthly"**
(choose the **"Day of month"**), a **"Time of day"**, and a
**"Timezone"**. For anything more intricate, tick **"Advanced (cron)"**
and enter standard 5-field cron syntax instead.

Scheduled reminders can also be scoped to who is around. Under
**"Only fire when…"**, choose **"always (system-wide)"** or
**"a specific member is fronting"** and pick the members. With member
scoping, if nobody on the list is fronting at the trigger time, you can
have the reminder queue and arrive as a digest when one of them next
starts fronting, capped at 5 missed firings; queued firings show on the
reminder's row.

The screenshot above shows both shapes: a daily evening reminder and a
weekly Sunday one, each riding an ntfy channel.

## Automated reminders

An **"After a switch"** reminder fires off front activity instead of the
clock. Choose the trigger under **"When"** (any member, or one specific
member) and **"Event"** (**"starts fronting"**, **"stops fronting"**, or
**"fronts or stops"**), then set **"Fire after (minutes)"** to delay the
ping past the front change itself.

The seeded example above fires 30 minutes after anyone starts fronting,
which suits check-in prompts like grounding after a switch.
