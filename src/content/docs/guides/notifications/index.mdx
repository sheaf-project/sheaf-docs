Watchers let trusted people get pinged when your fronts change. Each
watcher can have several delivery channels, and every channel has its own
triggers and per-member visibility filters, so you control exactly what
each recipient is allowed to see.

## Watchers and channels

The **"Notifications"** page has two tabs: **"Sending"** lists the
watchers you have set up for other people, and **"Receiving"** lists
notifications you get from other systems.

A watcher usually stands for one recipient, for example a partner or a
therapist. Under it you add channels, one per delivery method: web push to
a browser, mobile push to the Sheaf app, a webhook, ntfy, or Pushover.
Filters are set per channel, so the same person can get full detail on
their phone and a redacted ping somewhere shared.

## Add a watcher

1. Select **"Add watcher"** at the top of the page.
2. In the **"New watcher"** dialog, optionally fill in **"Label (optional)"**
   with who the watcher is for. The label is for your reference only;
   recipients never see it.
3. Confirm with Create.

<ThemeImage
	light={newWatcherLight}
	dark={newWatcherDark}
	alt="The New watcher dialog with the optional label field and its note that recipients never see the label"
/>

To remove a watcher later, use the **"Revoke watcher"** button on its
card and confirm with **"Revoke"**. All of its channels stop delivering.

## Add a channel

1. Select **"Channel"** on the watcher's card.
2. In the **"New channel"** dialog, give the channel a **"Name"**.
   Recipients do see this one; it identifies the subscription on their
   side.
3. Pick a **"Destination"**:
   - **"Web push (browser)"** sends notifications straight to the
     recipient's browser. You get a one-time activation link to pass on.
   - **"Mobile push (iOS + Android)"** rings the Sheaf app on every device
     the recipient has signed into. Also activated by link; the recipient
     needs the app installed and signed in.
   - **"Webhook"** POSTs each event to the **"URL"** you enter. The
     **"Payload format"** can be **"JSON (Sheaf format, signed)"** (add a
     **"Secret (optional)"** to get an HMAC signature header),
     **"Discord"**, **"Slack-compatible"**, or **"Plain text"**.
   - **"ntfy"** publishes to a **"Topic"** on the **"Server URL"** you
     choose (ntfy.sh by default, or your own server).
   - **"Pushover"** delivers via the recipient's Pushover **"User key"**.
     On instances that share one Pushover app, monthly delivery caps and a
     minimum debounce apply; expand the advanced section to paste your own
     Pushover app token and use your own quota instead.
4. Confirm to create the channel.

Webhook, ntfy, and Pushover channels are Active immediately.

## Activation links and channel states

Web push and mobile push channels start out Pending. Creating one shows a
one-time activation link, with **"Copy link"** and **"Open"** buttons.
The link is shown only once and expires, so copy it right away and relay
it to the recipient out-of-band.

The recipient opens the link on the **"Subscribe to notifications"**
page, selects **"Allow notifications"** (or hands off to the Sheaf app
for mobile push), and the channel turns Active. They do not need a Sheaf
account for this. If the link goes astray before it is used, open the
channel while it is still Pending and select **"Re-issue link"** to get a
fresh one.

Each channel row shows its state: Pending while awaiting activation,
Active while delivering, Disabled while paused. Use the pause and resume
controls on the row, or **"Pause"** and **"Resume"** on the channel's own
page, to stop and restart deliveries without deleting anything.

After subscribing, the recipient gets a **"Manage subscription"** link of
their own. It shows the channel's status and an **"Unsubscribe"** button,
again with no account needed. Unsubscribing is quiet: you see the channel
become disabled, but not who unsubscribed or when. If you pause a channel,
their manage page shows it as paused by the sender, and deliveries resume
automatically if you resume it.

## Triggers and filters

Selecting a channel opens its settings page. Changes apply when you save
them with the Save changes button at the bottom.

<ThemeImage
	light={channelDetailLight}
	dark={channelDetailDark}
	alt="The settings page for an active ntfy channel, with Pause, Send test, Duplicate, and Delete actions in the header, the destination and state card, the Triggers card with the co-fronter redaction dropdown, the Base set card, and the tops of the Group rules and Member rules cards"
/>

- **"Triggers"** chooses which events fire the channel:
  **"Member starts fronting"**, **"Member stops fronting"**, and
  **"Co-front composition changes"** (someone joins or leaves alongside a
  watched member). The co-front trigger has a **"Co-fronter redaction"**
  option so the ping can name co-fronters, give only a count, say just
  that someone joined, or be suppressed when anyone invisible to this
  channel is involved.
- **"Delivery"** controls how much a ping reveals and how often one can
  arrive. **"Payload sensitivity"** ranges from full (member names
  included) through minimal (someone started fronting) to bare (a front
  changed). **"Debounce (seconds)"** is the minimum gap between
  deliveries, **"Aggregation window (seconds)"** batches several events
  into one message (0 means realtime), and **"Quiet hours"** defers
  events inside a start-to-end window (in a timezone you pick) until the
  window ends.

**"Send test"** on the channel page delivers a test ping to an active
channel, and **"Duplicate"** copies a channel's whole configuration as a
starting point for a new one.

## Member visibility

Three cards on the channel page decide which members the channel may
report on. They layer up: **"Base set"** is Layer 1, **"Group rules"**
are Layer 2, and **"Member rules (overrides)"** are Layer 3. When the
layers disagree, the most specific one wins: a member override beats any
group rule, and a group rule beats the base set.

**"Base set"** (Layer 1) is the default for every member no other layer
mentions:

- **"All members"** starts from everyone, except that private members
  stay out unless you also turn on **"Also include private members"**.
- **"No-one (use Layer 2/3 to include)"** starts from nobody, for
  channels that should only report on members you list explicitly.

**"Group rules"** (Layer 2) adjust the base set by group. Pick a group
under **"Add group"**, then set its rule to **"Include"** or
**"Exclude"**. A rule applies to every member of the group, and if a
member's groups match both kinds of rule at once, the exclude wins.
Include rules also carry their own privacy setting for the private
members of the group: **"Privacy: inherit Layer 1"** follows the base
set's private-members switch, **"Privacy: include private"** brings them
in, and **"Privacy: exclude private"** keeps them out.

**"Member rules (overrides)"** (Layer 3) pin individual members. Pick a
member under **"Add member"** and choose **"Include"** or **"Exclude"**;
that decision is final, whatever the base set and group rules say. An
include here admits a private member too.

The **"Resolved members"** card at the bottom of the page previews the
outcome live while you edit, before anything is saved. It lists who will
and will not trigger the channel, and tags each member with the layer
that decided it: "L1 (private excluded)" for a private member kept out
by the base set, "L2" plus the group's name for a group rule, or
"L3 rule" for a member override.

For example, to report on everyone except one group, with a single
exception from that group:

1. Leave the base set on **"All members"**.
2. Add the group under **"Group rules"** and set it to **"Exclude"**.
3. Add the exception under **"Member rules (overrides)"** and set them
   to **"Include"**.

The preview then shows the group's members as excluded by the Layer 2
rule, the exception as included by their Layer 3 override, and any
private members outside the group still excluded at Layer 1 until you
switch them on.

## Receiving

The **"Receiving"** tab is the other direction: notifications other
systems send to you. If you redeem an activation link while signed in to
your account, the channel is listed here with the sending system's label
and its state, so you can **"Unsubscribe"** from one place instead of
keeping management links around.

If you use mobile push, a **"Your devices"** card lists each phone
registered from the Sheaf app. You can rename a device, mute it
temporarily without unregistering, or remove it entirely.
