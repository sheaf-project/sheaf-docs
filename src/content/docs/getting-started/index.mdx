Sheaf tracks fronting, members, journals, and more for plural systems - on
the web, your phone, and your wrist. It is open source and self-hostable,
and these docs cover every platform side by side.

## Hosted or self-hosted

Every Sheaf account lives on one instance. The hosted instance run by the
Sheaf project is at [app.sheaf.sh](https://app.sheaf.sh); anyone can also
run their own (see the
[self-hosting guide](https://github.com/sheaf-project/sheaf/blob/main/docs/SELFHOSTING.md)).
On the web you simply visit your instance's address in a browser. On mobile
the server URL matters: the apps ask which instance to sign in to, so if
your account is on a self-hosted instance you will need its URL, not
app.sheaf.sh.

New here? [Create an account](/getting-started/creating-an-account/) on
your instance, then [sign in](/getting-started/signing-in/).

## A quick tour

After signing in you land on the **"Dashboard"**, with the main areas of
Sheaf in the sidebar on the left.

<ThemeImage
	light={tourDashboardLight}
	dark={tourDashboardDark}
	alt="Dashboard after signing in, with the sidebar listing the main areas from Dashboard to Settings, the Currently fronting and System cards, and the Start front button"
/>

- **"Dashboard"**: who is **"Currently fronting"** right now, member and
  active-front counts, and the **"Start front"** button.
- **"Members"**: the members of your system - add them, edit their
  profiles, and archive the ones you no longer track.
- **"Journals"**: journal entries, written by a member or for the system
  as a whole.
- **"Fronts"**: your full front history, where past entries can be edited
  or deleted.
- **"Analytics"**: fronting statistics over time.
- **"Groups"**: organize members into groups; groups can be nested.
- **"Relationships"**: how members and groups relate to each other, shown
  as a graph you can explore and edit.
- **"Notifications"**: notification channels, and watchers who get pinged
  when the front changes.
- **"Reminders"**: scheduled reminders and automated ones that fire after
  a front change, delivered through your notification channels.
- **"Polls"**: let your headmates vote on a question.
- **"Messages"**: per-member message walls.
- **"Sharing"**: your public profile and share links, and who can see
  what. This only appears if your instance allows public profiles (or if
  you still have something shared from before it was turned off).
- **"Settings"**: system behaviour, safety, appearance, account,
  relationship types, and data options.

The sidebar also has a **"Support"** link just above **"Settings"**, and
instance admins see an extra **"Admin"** section below the main areas.

## Getting the app

Sheaf for Android is on the Play Store. If you prefer a build with no
Google dependencies, download the `.open` variant from the
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/sheaf-project/android/releases)
instead.

## A quick tour

After signing in you land on the home tab. The navigation bar along the
bottom of the screen holds **Home**, up to three areas you choose, and
**"More"**, which opens a menu listing every area of the app. You can
also open that menu by swiping in from the edge of the screen. On
tablets and other wide screens, the bar sits down the side instead.

<ThemeImage
	light={androidHomeLight}
	dark={androidHomeDark}
	alt="Home screen with the Welcome header and its settings icon, a current fronting card, the Switch button, the Quick switch row, and the bottom navigation from Home to More"
/>

- **Home**: who is fronting right now and for how long, the
  **"Switch"** button (bottom right), and the **"Quick switch"** row -
  tap a member there to switch to them instantly.
- **"Members"**: the members of your system and their groups - add them,
  edit their profiles, and organize them into groups.
- **"Front history"**: your full front history, with fronting analytics a
  tap away.
- **"Journals"**: journal entries, written by a member or for the system
  as a whole.

Those three are the defaults. To choose which areas sit in the bar and in
what order, go to **"Settings"** > **"Appearance"** > **"Navigation bar"**.

The **"More"** menu lists those areas too, plus:

- **"Groups"** and **"Analytics"**.
- **"Board messages"**: per-member message walls.
- **"Polls"**: let your headmates vote on a question.
- **"Reminders"**: scheduled reminders and automated ones that fire
  when a member starts or stops fronting.
- **"Relationships"**: relationship types, and a graph of how members
  and groups relate to each other.
- **"Files"**: files you have uploaded.
- **"Sharing"**: your public profile and share links. This only appears
  if your instance allows public profiles (or if you still have something
  shared from before it was turned off).
- **"Notifications"**: notification channels and subscriptions, your
  devices, the fronting notification, and app lock.
- **"Support"** and **"Settings"**.

The top of the home tab greets you by your system name, next to a
**"Settings"** gear icon.

**"Settings"** holds configuration: your system profile, account,
appearance, fronting, system options (including safety), data and
imports, notifications and app lock, server, and - for instance admins -
the admin tools. It also has **"Support"** (contact, status, and
policies), **"Danger Zone"** (sign out and account deletion), and
**"About"** at the bottom, which shows the app's version and build and
the server you are connected to, with the version it reports. That is
useful to have to hand when asking for support.
