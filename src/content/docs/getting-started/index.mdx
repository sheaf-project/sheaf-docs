Sheaf tracks fronting, members, journals, and more for plural systems - on
the web, your phone, and your wrist. It is open source and self-hostable,
and these docs cover every platform side by side.

## Hosted or self-hosted

Every Sheaf account lives on one instance. The hosted instance run by the
Sheaf project is at [app.sheaf.sh](https://app.sheaf.sh); anyone can also
run their own (see the
[self-hosting guide](https://github.com/sheaf-project/sheaf/blob/main/docs/SELFHOSTING.md)).
On the web you simply visit your instance's address in a browser. On mobile
the server URL matters: the apps ask which instance to sign in to, so if
your account is on a self-hosted instance you will need its URL, not
app.sheaf.sh.

New here? [Create an account](/getting-started/creating-an-account/) on
your instance, then [sign in](/getting-started/signing-in/).

## A quick tour

After signing in you land on the **"Dashboard"**, with the main areas of
Sheaf in the sidebar on the left.

<ThemeImage
	light={tourDashboardLight}
	dark={tourDashboardDark}
	alt="Dashboard after signing in, with the sidebar listing the main areas from Dashboard to Settings, the Currently fronting and System cards, and the Start front button"
/>

- **"Dashboard"**: who is **"Currently fronting"** right now, member and
  active-front counts, and the **"Start front"** button.
- **"Members"**: the members of your system - add them, edit their
  profiles, and archive the ones you no longer track.
- **"Journals"**: journal entries, written by a member or for the system
  as a whole.
- **"Fronts"**: your full front history, where past entries can be edited
  or deleted.
- **"Analytics"**: fronting statistics over time.
- **"Groups"**: organize members into groups; groups can be nested.
- **"Notifications"**: notification channels, and watchers who get pinged
  when the front changes.
- **"Reminders"**: one-off and automated reminders, delivered through your
  notification channels.
- **"Polls"**: let your headmates vote on a question.
- **"Messages"**: per-member message walls.
- **"Settings"**: system behaviour, safety, appearance, account, and data
  options.

The sidebar also has a **"Support"** link, and instance admins see an
extra **"Admin"** section below the main areas.
