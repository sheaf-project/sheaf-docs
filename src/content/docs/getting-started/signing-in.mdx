You sign in on the instance your account lives on - see
[Getting started](/getting-started/) if you are not sure which that is,
or [Creating an account](/getting-started/creating-an-account/) if you do
not have one yet.

## Sign in

1. Open your instance in a browser. While signed out you land on the
   login page.
2. On the **"Login"** tab, enter your **"Email"** and **"Password"**.
3. Select the sign-in button. If the instance runs a captcha on sign-in,
   complete it first; the button stays disabled until you do.

## Two-factor authentication

If your account has 2FA enabled, submitting your email and password brings
up a **"2FA code"** field (**"6-digit code or recovery code"**). Enter the
current code from your authenticator app and sign in again. If you have
lost the authenticator, one of your recovery codes works in the same
field.

To skip the prompt on a device you trust, tick
**"Trust this device for 30 days. Skip the 2FA prompt on this browser until then."**
before signing in. Once the checkbox is ticked, a
**"Device name (optional)"** box appears where you can give the device a
label you will recognise later.

## Forgot your password

If the instance has email delivery set up, a **"Forgot password?"** link
appears under the sign-in button.

1. Select it to open the **"Reset your password"** page, enter your
   **"Email"**, and send the reset link. You will see
   **"If an account with that email exists, we've sent a password reset
   link. Check your inbox."**
2. Open the link from the email. It lands on the **"Choose a new
   password"** page. If you open the page without the link, paste the
   token from the email into the **"Reset token"** field instead.
3. Enter a new **"Password"** (with the same confirm field and strength
   meter as registration) and select **"Reset password"**.
4. On **"Password reset"**, select **"Go to login"** and sign in with the
   new password. If the token was expired or already used, select
   **"Request a new link"** and start over.

## Sign in

The app signs in in two steps: first the server, then your account.

1. On **"Where's your Sheaf server?"**, enter your instance under
   **"Server URL or domain"**, or leave it blank to use the hosted
   instance (app.sheaf.sh). `https://` is added automatically. The server
   has to use https: the app refuses an `http://` address, because
   Android blocks unencrypted connections. If your instance is served
   under a path, include it (for example `example.org/sheaf`).
   Select **"Continue"**.
2. On the next step, a chip at the top shows the server you chose - tap
   it to go back and change it. With **"Sign In"** selected (not
   **"Register"**), enter your **"Email"** and **"Password"**, then
   select the **"Sign In"** button.

## Two-factor authentication

If your account has 2FA enabled, submitting your email and password
brings up the **"Two-factor authentication"** step. Enter the 6-digit
code from your authenticator app in the **"Authenticator code"** field
and select **"Verify"**. If you have lost the authenticator, tap
**"Use a recovery code instead"** and enter one of your recovery codes in
the **"Recovery code"** field, with or without its hyphen;
**"Use an authenticator code instead"** switches back. To skip the
prompt on this device, tick **"Remember this device for 30 days"** before
verifying; **"Back to sign in"** returns you to the email and password step.
